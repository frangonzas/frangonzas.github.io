Kolechat Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

1. Purpose

Kolechat is a communication and organization app intended primarily for parents, guardians and authorized members of school communities. It has been designed around data minimization, privacy and local-first operation.

2. Information you provide

Kolechat may process information you voluntarily provide, including a display name or family alias, messages, school and group information, events, birthday invitations, lost-and-found notices, community safety alerts, reports and other content you intentionally share through the app.

An optional telephone number may be entered during identity creation. The raw telephone number is used locally as part of the identity-generation process and is not intended to be displayed to other Kolechat users.

3. Technical identifiers

Kolechat creates pseudonymous identifiers and cryptographic keys used to identify app participants, protect supported communications and manage school-community membership.

4. Local storage

Kolechat stores app information locally on the device so supported functionality can remain available without a continuous Internet connection.

5. Peer-to-peer communication

When Local Network permission is granted, Kolechat may discover and communicate directly with nearby devices running Kolechat using Apple networking technologies. Local network access is used for Kolechat discovery and synchronization and is not intended for advertising or cross-app tracking.

6. iCloud and CloudKit

Where available, Kolechat uses Apple CloudKit to provide supported remote synchronization and identity-recovery functionality. Some information may therefore be transmitted to and stored within Apple's CloudKit infrastructure. Supported information is cryptographically protected according to its communication scope before remote transmission.

7. Sign in with Apple

Kolechat may offer Sign in with Apple as an optional identity mechanism. The core local app experience does not require Sign in with Apple.

8. How information is used

Information is processed only as necessary to provide Kolechat functionality, including communication, synchronization, membership management, event organization, private invitations, lost-and-found features, safety alerts, moderation, security and identity recovery when requested.

9. Advertising and tracking

Kolechat does not sell personal information to advertisers and is not designed to use cross-app tracking for advertising purposes.

10. User-generated content and safety

Kolechat applies a local preventive filter intended to stop clearly objectionable language from being published. Users can report messages, events, lost-and-found posts, alerts and members, and can block abusive users. Community administrators have moderation tools for responding to reports.

11. Children

Kolechat is intended primarily for parents, guardians and other authorized adult members of school communities. It is not designed for children to independently create accounts or directly participate as users. Users should take special care when sharing information relating to minors.

12. Community safety alerts

Kolechat safety-alert functions are supplementary community communication tools only. Kolechat is not an emergency service and does not replace contacting police, emergency medical services or other competent authorities. In Spain and the European Union, call 112 in an emergency.

13. Account and identity deletion

Users can initiate complete deletion directly inside Kolechat from Profile → Privacy and Account → Delete account and identity. The app deletes the local Kolechat identity, local cryptographic identity material, local app data and notifications. When CloudKit is available, Kolechat also requests deletion of CloudKit records created by that pseudonymous identity. If CloudKit is temporarily unavailable, the app retains only the minimum pseudonymous identifier needed to retry remote deletion when the service becomes available.

Content already received and stored independently on another user's device may require that device to synchronize before deletion state is reflected.

14. Security

Kolechat uses security measures including Apple's CryptoKit technologies and cryptographic mechanisms designed to protect supported communications and identities. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

15. Apple services

Kolechat relies primarily on Apple technologies including iOS, iCloud, CloudKit, Push Notifications, Network.framework and Sign in with Apple where applicable. Use of those services is also subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

16. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Kolechat evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and, where appropriate, notifying users through the app or website.

17. Contact

For privacy questions, support requests or data-related requests, use the official Kolechat Support page.
