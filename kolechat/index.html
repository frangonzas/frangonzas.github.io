School families connected

A simpler, more private way for school families to stay connected.

Kolechat brings school communities, chat, events, birthdays, groups, lost & found and important community alerts together in one intuitive iPhone app.

Community first

Create or join a school community, communicate with other families and organize everyday school life from one dedicated space.

Nearby and remote

Kolechat combines local peer-to-peer communication with Apple CloudKit synchronization for supported remote features.

Privacy by design

Personal phone numbers are not displayed to other members. Kolechat follows a local-first architecture and uses cryptographic protection for supported communication.

Made for families

Events, birthdays, classes, activity groups, lost items and community alerts are organized around the real needs of school families.

Need help?

Visit Kolechat Support for assistance, technical questions or privacy-related requests.
