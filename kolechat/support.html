Kolechat Support

Help for school communities, invitations, peer-to-peer connectivity, CloudKit synchronization, privacy, account deletion, blocking, reporting and other Kolechat features.

Community access

Users can create a school community, discover one nearby or join remotely with a valid Kolechat invitation pass.

Connectivity

For nearby discovery, make sure Local Network access is enabled for Kolechat in iOS Settings. Remote synchronization also requires an Internet connection and an available iCloud account.

Privacy & safety

Kolechat does not display personal telephone numbers to other community members. The app includes preventive content filtering plus reporting and blocking tools available directly from user-generated content.

Delete account and identity

Open Kolechat → Profile → Privacy and Account → Delete account and identity. The deletion flow is completed in the app and does not require contacting support.

Technical issues

When reporting a problem, include your iPhone model, iOS version, Kolechat version and a short description of what happened.

Contact support

You can contact the developer through GitHub for technical support, privacy questions or abuse reports.

Open a support request

Do not publish passwords, recovery codes, private invitation codes or cryptographic keys in a public support request.

Emergency situations

Kolechat is not an emergency service. Community safety alerts are supplementary communication tools only. In an emergency, contact the appropriate authorities or emergency services directly.
